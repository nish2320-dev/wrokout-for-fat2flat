# Privacy Policy — Fat2Flat Fitness

_Effective date: [DATE]_

Fat2Flat Fitness ("the app", "we") is a workout and meal tracker. This policy
explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version:
**everything you enter stays on your device. We don't collect it, see it,
sell it or share it.**

## What the app stores

When you use the app, it saves the following **only on your device**:

- Workouts you log: exercises, sets, reps, weights and times
- Meals you log: names, calories and protein
- Body-weight entries
- Your profile settings: the name you choose for the greeting, your daily
  calorie and protein goals, and your preferred units (lb or kg)

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app has no account or sign-in, and it doesn't connect to any
server run by us or anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising,
tracking or crash-reporting tools. We don't use your location, contacts,
photos, camera, microphone or Apple Health data.

Because your data never leaves your device, we can't see it, and we can't
recover it for you if it's lost.

## Device backups

Your iPhone may include the app's data in your iCloud or computer backups,
depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple and
covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not
by us. We have no access to them.

## Deleting your data

You're in control of your data at all times:

- **In the app:** Profile → **Erase all data** permanently deletes every
  workout, meal and weigh-in on the device.
- **Deleting the app** removes all of its data from the device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no
personal information, it collects none from children.

## Health information

The app is a personal log, not a medical device. Calorie, protein and
workout suggestions are general guidance, not medical advice. Talk to a
doctor before starting a new exercise or diet program.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting or sharing data, we'll update this policy
and change the effective date above **before** that version of the app is
released. Material changes will also be mentioned in the App Store release
notes.
