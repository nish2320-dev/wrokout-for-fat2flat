# Privacy Policy — Fat2Flat Fitness

_Effective date: version 2.0 release (update this date when 2.0 goes live)_

Fat2Flat Fitness ("the app", "we") is a workout and meal tracker. This policy
explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version:
**by default, everything you enter stays on your device.** The only things
that ever leave your phone are a barcode or food name you choose to look up
(see "Food lookups") and, **only if you choose to join a coach**, the progress
you share with that coach (see "Sharing with a coach").

## What the app stores

When you use the app, it saves the following on your device (and, only if you join a coach, shares a copy with them, as described below):

- Workouts you log: exercises, sets, reps, weights and times
- Meals you log: names, calories and protein
- Body-weight entries, body measurements (waist, hips, chest) and any
  progress photos you take or choose
- Your Transformation plan, if you start one: the age, sex, height, weights
  and activity level you enter to calculate your targets
- Your profile settings: the name you choose for the greeting, your daily
  calorie and protein goals, and your preferred units (lb or kg)

## What we collect

**If you don't join a coach: nothing.** You don't need an account, and the
app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We
don't use your location, contacts, microphone or Apple Health data.

## Sharing with a coach (optional)

If a personal trainer gives you their coach code, you can choose to share your
progress with them (Profile → Join my coach). To do this you sign in with
Apple, and the following is stored on our database provider, Supabase, so your
coach can see it:

- Your Apple account ID and the email Apple gives us (which can be a private
  relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"), and the name you enter
- Your workouts, weigh-ins, body measurements, meals and calorie and protein
  totals, and your Transformation plan and targets
- Your progress photos, **only if you turn on "Share progress photos"**
- Notes your coach sends you, and a notification address so they reach your
  phone

**Only your coach can see your shared data.** It is protected so that no other
user, coach or client can read it. We don't sell it, use it for advertising,
or share it with anyone else. Supabase stores it on our behalf under its own
security and privacy terms (supabase.com/privacy).

If you are a coach, the name you enter and your coach code are stored, so your
clients can join you and see who their notes are from.

You can turn photo sharing off at any time (shared photos are then deleted
from our database), stop sharing with your coach (Profile → Stop sharing,
which deletes your photos from our database and removes your coach's access),
or delete your account (see "Deleting your data").

## Food lookups

When you **scan a barcode** or **search for a food online**, the app sends the
barcode number or the words you searched for to
[Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a free, nonprofit food
database, to get calories and protein back. Nothing else is sent — not your
name, your meals, your workouts or any other app data. As with any website,
Open Food Facts' servers can see your device's internet (IP) address when it
answers; their handling of that is covered by the
[Open Food Facts privacy policy](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy).

Searching the built-in list of common foods, and typing calories in yourself,
happen entirely on your device.

## Camera and progress photos

The camera is used to read food barcodes and, if you choose, to take progress
photos at your weekly check-ins. Barcode scanning takes no photos. You can also
pick progress photos from your photo library; the app only receives the
photos you choose.

**Progress photos are stored inside the app on your iPhone.** They are only
uploaded if you join a coach and turn on "Share progress photos", and then
only your coach can see them. You can delete a check-in and its photos at any
time, and "Erase all data" removes them from your phone. You can turn camera or
photo access off in iOS Settings → Fat2Flat Fitness.

## Notifications

If you allow it, the app sends a reminder when your rest between sets is over,
and, if you are on the Transformation, reminders for your next workout, weekly
check-in and weigh-ins. These are scheduled on your device. If you join a
coach, notes from your coach are also delivered as notifications through
Apple's and Expo's push notification services, which see only the note's text
and your device's notification address. You can turn notifications off in iOS
Settings → Fat2Flat Fitness → Notifications, and the app keeps working.

## Device backups

Your iPhone may include the app's data in your iCloud or computer backups,
depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple and
covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not
by us. We have no access to them.

## Deleting your data

You're in control of your data at all times:

- **In the app:** Profile → **Erase all data** permanently deletes every
  workout, meal, weigh-in, check-in and progress photo on the device. Single
  check-ins can be deleted from the check-in screen.
- **Your account:** Profile → **Delete account** permanently deletes your
  account and everything stored online for it: shared progress, photos, notes
  and, for coaches, the coach code and client list.
- **Deleting the app** removes all of its data from the device. (If you have
  an account, delete it in the app first, or email us and we'll delete it.)

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13.

## Health information

The app is a personal log, not a medical device. Calorie, protein and
workout suggestions, including the Transformation plan and its coach, are
general guidance, not medical advice. The details you enter for your plan are
used on your iPhone to calculate them, and shared only with a coach you join. Talk to a doctor before starting a
new exercise or diet program.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting or sharing data, we'll update this policy
and change the effective date above **before** that version of the app is
released. Material changes will also be mentioned in the App Store release
notes.
